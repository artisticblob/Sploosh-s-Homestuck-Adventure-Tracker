The purpose of this website is to, as the name suggests, track my notes and thoughts as I read through hit 2013 webcomic Homestuck by Andrew Hussie.

I'm choosing to do this in website format for a few reasons:

  1. The comic is a whopping 8,000 pages long minimum (at least to my knowledge). I am, inevitably, going to lose my place in the comic or forget what happened, so if I make new entries here, they'll provide... some insight, at least.
  2. I wanna keep my web-dev skills strong outside of my HTML and JavaScript class, and I figure this'd be a fun way of upkeeping it.
  3. A pretty common theme of Homestuck, at least in its earlier pages, is weirdly gimmick-y tech (think the Captchalogue or the Classpect system (or, y'know, the concept of SBurb as a whole)), so I think it'd be entertainingly on-brand to make my chosen note-taking place be a website's code of all places.

I also just generally think it'd be fun to have a website dedicated to this; Homestuck has always partially interested me due to Toby Fox's work on the soundtrack and how much of Homestuck/Hussie's humor bled into Toby's games UNDERTALE and deltarune, and otherwise I get to share things I'm having fun with related to this comic and world like the "Craftbound" Minecraft modpack based on Homestuck.
